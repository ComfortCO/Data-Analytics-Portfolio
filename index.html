Sales Performance & KPI Analysis Dashboard

Demo Project

This project shows an interactive sales performance and KPI dashboard designed to support data-driven decision-making across products, retailers, regions, and time periods. The dashboard tracks key business metrics including total sales, quantity sold, number of retailers, and performance against targets, providing stakeholders with a clear view of overall performance at a glance. Monthly sales trends highlight seasonality and growth patterns, while retailer, brand, and regional analyses identify top-performing channels and products. Interactive filters enable users to drill down by brand and sales representative, allowing for flexible exploration of the data. This analysis supports strategic planning by identifying revenue drivers, underperforming segments, and opportunities for optimisation in sales and distribution.
